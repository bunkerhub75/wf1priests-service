House Rules

How every dish is prepared

We follow the safety and safeguarding guidelines of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and a few kitchen rules of our own.

Two adults, every time

At least two adult leaders are present at every service activity. Priests serve in pairs or more and never one-on-one with an adult or child.

General Handbook 20.7.1

Someone's home

For service inside a home, a member of the household stays home while we work. Childcare happens at the meetinghouse or with a parent present.

Children and Youth Protection training

Adults run the big equipment

Ladders above a step stool, pressure washers, leaf blowers and power tools are run by an adviser. No roof work and no chainsaws for priests.

Quorum standard · General Handbook 20.7.6

Safe rides

Only licensed adult drivers, in safe vehicles, with a seat belt for everyone. The bishop approves any travel.

General Handbook 20.7.7

Parents in the loop

Parents know where their sons are serving. Anything with higher-than-ordinary risk needs a signed Permission and Medical Release form.

General Handbook 20.7.4

The sacrament, by authorization

Priests take the sacrament to homebound members only when the bishop authorizes it. Requests for it go to the bishop.

General Handbook 18.9.1

Weeknights & Saturdays

Service activities aren't scheduled on Sunday, apart from the sacrament for the homebound when the bishop assigns it.

General Handbook 20.5.8

No tips, please

Our service is free. A thank-you note is plenty, though no priest has ever turned down a cookie.
